# VCRX | Option C brand kit

Built from the approved angular VCRX wordmark concept.

## Files
- logos/: primary wordmark, cropped transparent version, standalone V mark, horizontal descriptor lockup
- icons/: dark and orange square app icon variants
- tv/: 16:9 landscape Apple TV-style tiles (1920×1080, 1280×720) and a transparent variant
- reference/: approved concept and brand overview

## Colors
- Primary orange: `#FF5F00`
- Black: `#000000`

## Typography suggestions
- Display: Rajdhani Bold / Semibold
- Interface: Inter Regular / Medium

## Tagline
Your media. Your hardware. Your rules.

## Notes
- The Apple TV-style rectangle is a **landscape promotional/tvOS-style visual**, not a claim of Apple platform approval.
- Assets are PNG raster artwork derived from the selected design concept, not precision-redrawn vector master files.
- The project name has not been formally cleared for trademark use.
