# Live TV, planned 2026-10-31

Pete asked for "iptv with full set of iptv channels if you think this fits."
It fits: Live TV and DVR were cut from the MVP (`docs/REFERENCE.md` section
15) alongside the web app and music, and both of those have since shipped. It
is Plex's next missing piece, and the HLS pipeline built for Safari (#8) does
most of the streaming work already. It goes after the 2026-10-31 feature
batch, as milestone 11.

## What "every channel" can and cannot mean

VCRX plays channels from sources the admin configures. It never ships, bundles
or recommends a channel list. Same reasoning as tape trading (PLAN.md): the
project stays a player, never a source.

- **Fits:** an antenna through an HDHomeRun tuner (local broadcast channels,
  free and legal to record), free ad-supported channels that publish their own
  playlists, and any M3U playlist with an XMLTV guide that the admin has the
  right to watch. iptv-org's public index of free-to-air streams can be
  pointed at by the admin; VCRX does not curate it.
- **Does not fit:** the paid "every channel for $15 a month" IPTV services.
  They are nearly always unlicensed restreams. VCRX will play an M3U the admin
  supplies, but no doc, default or picker will point at one.
- **Cannot be done:** YouTube TV, Sling, Hulu Live and the like. They are DRM
  apps with no playlist or API, so they stay in their own apps on the TV.

## Build order (one PR each, each gated green)

1. **Sources and channels.** `live_tv.sources` in `vcrx.yaml`: HDHomeRun
   (discovered over its local HTTP API, `lineup.json`), or an M3U URL or file.
   A migration adds `live_sources` and `channels` (number, name, logo cached
   locally, enabled flag). Admin page to enable, renumber and hide channels.
   Channel access is a grant like a library: off for friends and kid
   profiles until granted.
2. **Guide.** XMLTV from a URL or file, refreshed every 6 hours, 14 days kept,
   matched to channels by `tvg-id`. An HDHomeRun's own guide needs
   SiliconDust's paid DVR service, so an antenna setup takes XMLTV from
   Schedules Direct (about $35 a year) or a free grabber. The server sends a
   guide source nothing but the request.
3. **Watching live.** ffmpeg pulls the channel and feeds the existing HLS
   segmenter with a sliding live playlist instead of the growing EVENT one.
   Two viewers on one channel share one pull. A tuner limit per source (an
   HDHomeRun has 2 to 4) says "all tuners busy" rather than failing. Live
   watching writes Activity but never watch state.
4. **Clients.** Web: a Live TV page with a now and next grid. TV app: a Live
   row on Home, a full guide, and channel up and down in the player.
5. **DVR (later, own decision).** Record a program or a series to a
   recordings folder, which a normal TV library then scans. Needs the first
   writable media mount (today every media mount is read-only), so it waits
   for Pete's yes on that.

Every new route gets its access walk probe (#35), and the stream URLs never
reach a client: clients only see VCRX's own HLS.

## Decided 2026-10-31: playlists only

Pete: "there's no antenna tv in ithaca ny." Live TV is M3U playlists with an
XMLTV guide. HDHomeRun discovery is out of steps 1 and 2; it comes back only
if a tuner ever does (one at a house with reception, streamed over the
friends' servers link, is the shape that would fit).

The network affiliates of a market are not on any lawful free playlist: their
streaming is sold by retransmission consent, and Aereo (2014) and Locast
(2021) lost trying otherwise. The live TV services that carry them are DRM
apps, so they stay in their own apps on the TV, as above.

## Status

1. **Sources and channels: built** (`server/internal/live`, migration 0017).
   `live_tv.sources` in vcrx.yaml, each an http(s) URL or an absolute file
   path, or `m3u_url_file` for a URL with credentials in it. Read at startup
   and every `refresh_hours` (12). A channel is matched across refreshes by
   tvg-id, else a hash of its stream URL; the admin's number and enabled flag
   survive a refresh, and a channel the playlist drops is marked gone, not
   deleted. Limits: 32 MB a playlist, 20,000 channels, 1 MB a logo. Logos are
   cached from public addresses only, checked on the address each connection
   dials, because a playlist the admin did not write names them. The Live TV
   grant is `allow_live_tv` on the user's policy: off for everyone but admins,
   never for a lent tape's borrower, and the access walk probes both routes
   as a user, a profile, an invited device and a linked server. The admin web
   page (enable, renumber) comes with step 4's web client.
2. Guide: next.
